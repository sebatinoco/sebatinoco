## About Me 🤗

- 💙 **Interests:** Autonomous Agents, Recommender Systems, Natural Language Processing, and MLOps. I'm also passionate about teaching and mentoring.
- 🎓 **Education:** I hold a Master's degree in Data Science from the [University of Chile](https://www.dcc.uchile.cl/), where I was supervised by [Andrés Abeliuk](https://aabeliuk.github.io).
- 🔬 **Research:** Former member of the [RELELA](https://relela.com/) (Representations for Learning and Language) research group.
